Advertising Service

Reverse Graffiti
Advertising

Reverse graffiti (also called clean tagging or grime writing) is a form of eco-advertising where we selectively clean a dirty surface to reveal a crisp, high-contrast image or message — using nothing but water and our power sprayer.

No paint. No chemicals. No permits required in most areas. The "ad" naturally fades as the surrounding surface gets dirty again, making it a temporary, fully reversible, and eco-friendly marketing method.

Perfect for sidewalks, parking lots, driveways, concrete walls, and curbs. Great for grand openings, events, storefronts, and neighborhoods that want a memorable local presence.
